About this machine

The Kubota U27-4 is a 2,600 kg machine, the size most groundworks contractors buy first and keep longest. VMAX supplies it brand new with the attachment circuits piped and pressure tested in our workshop rather than left for a later visit, so a breaker, shear or tilt-rotate can go on the day it arrives. Machine control is fitted and calibrated on request, and every unit leaves on a scheduled service plan.

  • Twin auxiliary circuits, pressure tested before delivery
  • Machine-control ready with factory mounting points
  • Heavy-duty undercarriage with full-length track guards
  • Auto idle and boom float as standard
